Privacy Policy

PDF Maker Pro Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version

PDF Maker Pro works on your phone. Your documents are opened, edited, created and read on the device itself. They are never uploaded, and the app has no permission to use the internet at all.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting service. We do not receive your documents, their names, their contents, or any record that you used the app.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps the following on the device, where only it can read them:

  • Your library: a list of the documents you opened or created, with their names, sizes, page counts, reading positions and bookmarks.
  • Copies of documents you open or create, stored inside the app's private folder so they stay available after you close the file manager or share sheet that sent them.
  • Your settings: theme, layout and sort order.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so none of it is copied to Google Drive or to any other server.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Deleting a document in the app deletes the app's copy. Files you saved elsewhere, such as Downloads or your gallery, stay where you put them until you delete them.

Permissions

The app declares no Android permissions beyond one that Android generates for its own internal messaging. In particular it has no internet, storage, camera or location permission.

  • Documents are read through Android's own file picker, which gives the app access only to the file you choose.
  • Folders in the Browse tab are read through Android's folder picker, and only the folders you pick.
  • Photographs are taken through the camera app already on your phone.

Google Play services

Two features are provided by Google Play services, which is part of your phone rather than this app:

  • Document scanner: the scanning screen belongs to Google Play services. The first time you use it, Play services may download the scanner component. The app tells you this before the first scan. Your scan is processed on the device.
  • Text recognition (OCR): the recognition model is bundled inside the app and runs on the device. No image is uploaded.

Google's handling of Google Play services is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version collects anything, this policy will say so before that version is released, and the Data safety section of the Play listing will be updated to match. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
