Privacy Policy

PDF Maker Pro

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

Your documents stay on your phone. Every tool - reading, creating, merging, compressing, recognising text - runs on the device, and no file you open or make is ever uploaded.

The app does show ads, and ads are not free of data. To show them, Google's advertising service receives information about your device, described below. That is the only thing this app sends anywhere.

What the app collects

We collect nothing ourselves. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no crash reporting service. We never receive your documents, their names, their contents, or any record of what you did in the app.

Ads

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. To choose and show an ad, and to count it, AdMob receives:

  • The advertising ID of your device, an identifier Android provides for this purpose, which you can reset or delete in Android's settings.
  • Your device's IP address, which indicates your approximate location - the region or city, not a street.
  • Information the device reports about itself, such as model, operating system version and language.
  • What happens to the ad: that it was shown, and whether it was tapped.

AdMob may use this to choose which ads you see, including ads based on what you have done in other apps and websites. Google's own policy explains what it does with it, at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

Your documents are not part of this. Their contents and names are never sent to AdMob or to anyone else.

Your choice

Where the law requires it - in the UK and the European Economic Area - the app shows Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you decide whether ads can be personalised. Choosing no means you see non-personalised ads, not that the app stops working.

Anywhere in the world, Android lets you turn off personalised advertising or delete the advertising ID entirely, under Settings, usually in Privacy or Google services.

What stays on your phone

  • Your library: a list of the documents you opened or created, with their names, sizes, page counts, reading positions and bookmarks.
  • Copies of documents you open or create, stored inside the app's private folder so they stay available after you close the file manager or share sheet that sent them.
  • Page thumbnails the app draws of the first page of your documents.
  • Your settings: theme, layout and sort order.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so none of it is copied to Google Drive or to any other server.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Deleting a document in the app deletes the app's copy. Files you saved elsewhere, such as Downloads or your gallery, stay where you put them until you delete them.

Permissions

The app asks for two:

  • Internet and network state, used to load ads. Nothing else in the app uses the network, and no document is uploaded through it.

It does not ask for storage, camera or location.

  • Documents are read through Android's own file picker, which gives the app access only to the file you choose.
  • Folders in the Browse tab are read through Android's folder picker, and only the folders you pick.
  • Photographs are taken through the camera app already on your phone.

Google Play services

Two features are provided by Google Play services, which is part of your phone rather than this app:

  • Document scanner: the scanning screen belongs to Google Play services. The first time you use it, Play services may download the scanner component. The app tells you this before the first scan. Your scan is processed on the device.
  • Text recognition (OCR): the recognition model is bundled inside the app and runs on the device. No image is uploaded.

Google's handling of Google Play services is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone itself, and the ads it shows are not directed at children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version collects or sends anything else, this policy will say so before that version is released, and the Data safety section of the Play listing will be updated to match. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
